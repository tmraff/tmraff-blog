# Zines

I would define a zine as a work, typically self/small-press published and printed in small runs, where the creator has direct control over its contents.

It is hard to define what constitutes a zine. There are plenty of examples where definitions get shaky, such as with digital zines, zines published by organisations, zines that become professional (prozines), and so on. 

## Types of zine

There are several named types of zine. Here's a peerie glossary for you:

> [!definition] [[Fanzines\|Fanzine]]
> (Fan magazine)
> A zine about something the creator is a fan of. 

> [!definition] Perzine
> (Personal zine)
> A zine (typically) about the creator's personal experiences.

> [!definition] Apazine
> (Amateur press association zine)
> A zine created by a member of an APA and distributed to other members.

> [!definition] Compzine
> (Compilation zine)
> A zine created through compiling multiple folks submissions together.

> [!definition] Litzine
> (Literary zine)
> A zine featuring collections of essays, stories, or poetry. 

> [!definition] Minicomic
> (Miniature comics)
> A comic zine that may or may not be mini. 

> [!definition] [[Magic Zines\|Magic zine]]
> An eight page zine created by folding and cutting a single sheet of paper.

>[!definition] Prozine
> (Professional zine)
> A professionally produced magazine, typically aimed at a fandom.
