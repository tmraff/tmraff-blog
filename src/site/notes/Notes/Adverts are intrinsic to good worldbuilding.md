# Advertisements are intrinsic to good worldbuilding

The adverts in [[Cyberpunk 2077\|Cyberpunk 2077]] are intrinsic to my enjoyment of Night City. Everywhere I turn, my gaze is filled with large, colourful, sexual signs selling me something - Chromanticore, Watson Whore, Kiroshi Implants. Until I drive out into the Badlands, they’re impossible to escape. What I could not bear in reality fills me with awe in Night City. The visual distraction is what makes the world of Cyberpunk 2077 feel like it truly is an [[Capitalism\|ultra-capitalist]] [[Cyberpunk\|cyberpunk]] [[Dystopias\|dystopia]].

![Pasted image 20240802140207.png](/img/user/Notes/Pasted%20image%2020240802140207.png)

Outwith the futuristic context, adverts can still be implemented. Craftsmen holler about their wares, putting adverts in newspapers and on community message boards. Companies advertise on the radio. Graffiti is sprayed beneath bridges. Religious art is painted on chapel ceilings.
