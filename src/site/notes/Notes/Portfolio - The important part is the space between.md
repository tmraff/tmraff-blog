# The important part is the space between

Back in 2022 (when I was 19), I spent a lot of time making art about the various jelly species that live in Shetland waters. It culminated in an exhibition at Gaada's exhibition space outside of the Toogs Artist Workshop.

The exhibition consisted of a variety of risoprints of sketchbook pages, a linocut printed on hand-marbled paper, four large giclée prints of digital paintings I had created, and a flag with handsewn tendrils.
