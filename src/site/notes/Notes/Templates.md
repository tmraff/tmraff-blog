# Templates

As sharing is caring and I nab everyone else’s stuff all the time, I’ll add the Obsidian templates I use in Root Directory here as I go.

## Regular page

The vast majority of my notes use this template. I don’t always fill in every section, but I like having it there. I’ve been using it for several years and have become attached.

`created` and `updated` are both automatically populated by [Linter](https://github.com/platers/obsidian-linter); `title` is populated with the filename upon creation – it has to be manually updated if you later change it.

“Back to:” is populated with a list of links to broader topics, sort of like keywords in a journal article. I don’t like folders and neither am I taken by tags, so this is my solution to hierarchy.


```markdown
---
created: 
updated: 
title: {{title}}
---

Back to: [[]], 

# {{title}}



---

## Related to

-

## Ideas that stand in opposition

-

## Further reading

-

## References

- 

```
