# LONGBOi

_LONGBOi_ is my silly little zine series. I have so many things I have written or want to write about but can't be arsed writing a proper essay on. _LONGBOi_ lets me make this your problem anyway.

## Why LONGBOi?

The name _LONGBOi_ comes from a recurring discussion about long hot water bottles. I have a dinosaur one from B&M that accompanies me through my chronic pancreatitis flares and a visitor told me her daughter-in-law refers to hers as "Longboy". I have since heard the same from multiple other people. While Jono and I were making the first of these zines, he suggested it as a name. The styling is merely [[Internet Poisoning\|Internet Poisoning]]. 

## Philosophy

### Lazy masters

_LONGBOi_ zines are designed around the Lazy Master method. I can't find any information about this online and am thus compelled to attribute the term to Jono's circle of printmakers. Basically, you print the same file on both sides of a sheet of paper and when you slice it up, it has both the front and reverse on each zine. 

> [!example] Lazy Master Diagram
> ![LONGBOi - Lazy Master Example.png\|png-blue](/img/user/Notes/LONGBOi%20-%20Lazy%20Master%20Example.png)

This printing method is the reason why each _LONGBOi_ is 105mm by 297mm. Each A3 sheet gives four zines, or two per A4 sheet. 

### YAML

Each _LONGBOi_ begins with metadata because I find it funny. I chose YAML because it is human-readable, takes up little space, and I can just copy and paste it from my Obsidian notes.

### Typefaces

Typefaces typically used in a _LONGBOi_ include:

- [Orbitron](https://www.theleagueofmoveabletype.com/orbitron), designed by [Matt McInerney](https://www.matt.cc/) and licenced under [OFL](https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/).
- [JetBrains Mono](https://www.jetbrains.com/lp/mono/) and licenced under [OFL](https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/)
- [PicNic](https://www.velvetyne.fr/fonts/picnic/), designed by [Mariel Nils](https://www.instagram.com/mariel.nils) and licenced under [CUTE](https://genderfluid.space/documents/2024_BBB_CUTE-EN.pdf)

Occasionally, you will also find icons from [Font Awesome](https://fontawesome.com/)'s [free collection](https://fontawesome.com/search?ic=free-collection).

### Separations

_LONGBOi_ was created with Gaada's two-drum risograph in mind. Most issues are therefore printed with just two colours. I typically use blue and yellow. Some issues also include fluo pink. 

Once I've got everything set up and uploaded to [GitHub](https://github.com/tmraff/LONGBOi), you're welcome to print them in whichever colours you have access to.
