# Pocket notebooks are the anti-smartphone

I have seen many videos on YouTube with titles along the lines of ‘This pocket notebook saved me from infinite scrolling!’.

In these videos, the content creator usually holds a pocket catch-all notebook or a pocket commonplace book and talks about how they have been using it instead of their phone. Afterall, they’re roughly the same size.

Surprisingly, the anecdotal consensus is that using a pocket notebook can reduce your phone usage and reading through your own notes can be more fulfilling than mindless scrolling. It introduces a level of intentionality that isn’t present in digital procrastination.

It goes against the idea that [[We should be available at all times\|we should be available at all times]]. It tells us that not being connected, not being available, can actually be a positive thing. It gives us room to think.
